# Code of conduct

Abliteration welcomes respectful discussion of reproducible experiments, model support, documentation, and code. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and project spaces.

Be considerate of other contributors and their time. Discuss evidence and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing, spam, and sharing someone else's private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep project spaces useful and safe. For a concern that should not be posted publicly, use the [maintainer contact page](https://www.hackingdream.net/p/contact-us.html). Include links or a concise description; do not send credentials or private datasets.
